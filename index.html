GUIDE
How to play
🗺️ The goal
100 fictional student records are hidden across 7 islands. Each island hides one question that can only be answered by analyzing the data — download the CSV, dig into it with Excel/Sheets/Python/whatever you like, and find the one student who matches the clue.
Step by step
1. Click an unlocked island on the map (Island 1 opens first, the rest are 🔒 locked).
2. Read the mission clue carefully.
3. Tap ⬇ Download Island CSV to get the dataset.
4. Analyze it yourself and find the matching student's Reg_No (e.g. S042).
5. Type it into the answer box and hit Submit.
6. ✅ Correct unlocks the next island. ❌ Wrong just costs a few points — try again.
Scoring
✅ Correct Answer: +100
💡 Hint Used: −20 (optional, once per island)
❌ Wrong Answer: −10 (each wrong try)
⚡ Fast Completion Bonus: +50 (finish within the first half of the timer)
⏭ Stuck on one?
Hit Skip for now to move on to the next island without losing your progress. Skipped islands show up in an amber "Skipped Islands" list below the map — jump back in and solve them anytime before the clock runs out.
⏱ Timer & submission
You have 60 minutes to reach Treasure Island. When your run ends — win or time-out — your team name, score and time are sent automatically to the organizer. Nothing else to do.
